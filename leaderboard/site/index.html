Two results hold at once and quoting either alone misleads. On magnitude, naive comparison of the six-group score spread (MASD) against a two-replicate pairwise noise floor overstates disparity by up to 2.2461x through statistic arity alone (d2(6)/d2(2)). Against an arity-matched floor the observed-to-null ratio across 34 cells with a defined ratio runs 0.68 to 17.24 (median 0.94), and zero ratio intervals lie entirely above 1.0. On exchangeability, a within-set randomization test finds 8 of 24 family cells below p=0.05 unadjusted and 4 surviving Benjamini-Hochberg (smallest adjusted p = 0.0024): sonnet/hiring/C0+expanded, llama31-8b/hiring/C0+expanded, fable/hiring/C3+expanded, sonnet/hiring/C3+expanded. The survivor count is family-dependent: 4 under the family used, 6 over every cell, 3 under Benjamini-Yekutieli. The effect is small, roughly 0.3 of a call-to-call noise SD, and its direction is that white-male-coded names score lowest in every surviving cell, including the cross-vendor open-weights model. Read this as neither 'no bias' nor 'bias found'.
